About the workshops

Each workshop is short, practical and run by student mentors in the School of Life Sciences. You'll spend most of the time working on real examples with other students, with mentors on hand to help.

All materials are posted here, so you can read them on your phone or laptop, or use them with a screen reader. Paper copies are always available on the day.
