Scientific Report Writing

Learn what markers look for, then mark and fix a real lab report.

Wednesday 25 November · 4–6pm · JMS 2B4 Kroto Room

Sign up Workshop materials Slides

What happens on the day

  1. 4:10Talk, part 1: what markers look for in a lab report.
  2. 4:25Task 1: Be the marker. Each of you marks one section of a real report, then you put your scores together.
  3. 4:55The reveal. See the grade it actually got, and why.
  4. 5:10Short break.
  5. 5:15Talk, part 2: writing style, with a few quick try-it-yourself questions.
  6. 5:20Task 2: Fix the report. In small groups, rewrite sentences and spot the problems in a table and graph.
  7. 5:40Takeaways and feedback.
  8. 5:45Task 2 answers, then questions until 6pm.
